Last updated: November 5, 2026

VHabitat is a static website. It has no accounts, no comments and no forms, and it does not ask you for personal information.

## Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are read. When a page loads, Google Analytics sets cookies and sends Google information such as the page address, the referring page, your browser and device, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. The data is used only to understand how the site is used. Google handles it under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can stop it with your browser's tracking protection or the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the site, Cloudflare processes request information such as your IP address and browser, under the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Your choice of day or night pages

If you press the moon or the sun, the site remembers your choice in your browser's local storage under the name `bestiary-scheme`. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it.

## Links to other sites

Entries link to other websites. Their own privacy policies apply once you follow a link.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be asked through the [VHabitat issue tracker](https://github.com/ShortArrow/vhabitat/issues).
