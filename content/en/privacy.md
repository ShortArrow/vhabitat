VHabitat is a static website. It has no accounts, no comments and no forms, and it does not ask you for personal information.

## Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are read. When a page loads, Google Analytics sets cookies and sends Google information such as the page address, the referring page, your browser and device, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. The data is used only to understand how the site is used. Google handles it under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can stop it with your browser's tracking protection or the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

The site also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure how fast pages load. Cloudflare adds its script to the pages it serves, and the script reports timing measurements and the page address. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not collect or use visitors' personal data ([About](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/about/)). The Web Analytics script sets no cookies and stores nothing else in your browser, and Vitals Explorer, the part of Web Analytics that reports Core Web Vitals, does not fingerprint you by your IP address or browser ([Core Web Vitals](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/data-metrics/core-web-vitals/)). Cloudflare does not track individual visitors across the sites it serves ([Data origin and collection](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/data-metrics/data-origin-and-collection/)).

## Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the site, Cloudflare processes request information such as your IP address and browser, under the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Cloudflare also adds a bot-detection script, JavaScript Detections, to the pages it serves under shortarrow.jp. The script checks whether a visit comes from an automated program, and Cloudflare stores the result in your browser in a cookie named `cf_clearance` ([JavaScript Detections](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-challenges/challenge-types/javascript-detections/)). Cloudflare sets this cookie, not VHabitat. A site can use the result to block or challenge visits, but VHabitat has no rule that does, so the cookie blocks nothing here.

## Your choice of day or night pages

If you press the moon or the sun, the site remembers your choice in your browser's local storage under the name `bestiary-scheme`. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it.
